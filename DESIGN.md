## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for an elite international Motion Designer, Director, and Post-Production Creative Studio. Synthesizing avant-garde editorial publication formats (FRAMEFORM style, Japanese print zines, premium lookbooks) with the high-pressure precision of cinematic post-production control suites, the visual identity pairs raw structural clarity with uncompromising kinetic tension.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Cinematic & Uncompromising:** High-impact contrast between dense ink black and tactile warm cream, punctuated by an incendiary carmine red accent that signals active playback, recording cues, and vital interaction anchors.
- **FRAMEFORM Editorial Rigor:** Monolithic, heavyweight typography locked in strict all-caps title hierarchies, structured grid lines, hard offsets, and post-production telemetry indices (`TIMECODE`, `FPS 24`, `ASPECT 2.39:1`).
- **Tactile Materiality:** Evokes a hybrid between a high-grade darkroom inspection sheet, a film editing terminal, and an art-house production ledger.

### Target Audience
Global commercial directors, international advertising executives, film commissioners, luxury fashion houses, and progressive entertainment agencies seeking visionary motion direction, narrative weight, and flawless technical craftsmanship.

## Colors

The palette operates on a triadic, ultra-high-contrast editorial dynamic:

- **Primary (`#AB1509` / Carmine Punch):** The sole chromatic hero. Embodying recording tallies, active cues, hover snaps, play buttons, and editorial impact points. It is never muted or softened; it commands absolute focal dominance.
- **Secondary (`#0A0A0A` / Pitch Black):** Deep structural ink. Used for heavyweight display typography, sharp architectural borders, monolithic buttons, and full darkroom screening panels.
- **Tertiary (`#222222` / Graphite):** Serves technical telemetry indices, hair lines, structural coordinates, and secondary contextual rules.
- **Neutral (`#FFF7D3` / Vanilla Base):** The primary tactile canvas. Replaces cold digital whites with an organic, warm paper stock reminiscent of heavyweight publication grade paper.

### Functional Roles & Contrast
The relationship between `#FFF7D3` and `#0A0A0A` yields crisp legibility, while `#AB1509` punctures the neutral plane with cinematic intent. Alternating darkroom modules (inverse sections with `#0A0A0A` backdrops and `#FFF7D3` text) are leveraged for video screening areas, showreel showcases, and interactive playback suites.

## Typography

The typographic architecture is built strictly upon **Argentum Sans** across the entire digital ecosystem. Every heading and label executes the structural precision of FRAMEFORM design manuals.

### Typographic Rules & Hierarchy
- **Primary Hero & Section Titles (`display-xl`, `headline-lg`, `headline-md`):** Must strictly utilize `Argentum Sans Black` (weight 900 or 950) rendered in **mandatory uppercase (`text-transform: uppercase`)**. Tight editorial tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.04em`) creates continuous typographic bands, reminiscent of physical letterpress blocks and cinematic title cards.
- **Section Dividers & Case Titles (`headline-sm`):** Set in `Argentum Sans Bold` (700) or `SemiBold` (600) for disciplined editorial wayfinding.
- **Narrative & Editorial Copy (`body-lg`, `body-md`):** Set in `Medium` (500) and `Regular` (400) weights with optical line spacing, capped at a 65-character line length to preserve maximum legibility against both vanilla and pitch-black fields.
- **Telemetry & Production Labels (`label-technical`, `label-micro`):** Rendered in `Argentum Sans SemiBold` (600) in strict uppercase with expansive letter spacing (`0.14em` to `0.18em`), establishing tabular consistency for aspect ratios, camera specifications, client indices, and timecodes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical, architectural 12-column modular grid bounded by explicit hairline borders (`1px solid #0A0A0A` or `#222222`). Content blocks are not floating; they are locked into cell structures reminiscent of printing plates and timecode tracks.

### Multi-device Adaptation
- **Mobile (< 768px):** The grid folds into a monolithic single-column ledger. Screen margins lock at `1rem`, card dividers collapse vertically, and telemetry stamps stack into horizontal header bars above video canvases.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column split layout. Works-in-progress and project reels are rendered in alternating 2-column or 3-column matrices with persistent horizontal separator rules.
- **Desktop & Widescreen (> 1024px):** A dynamic 12-column broadsheet configuration. Supports asymmetrical compositions, such as a 7-column interactive preview pane accompanied by a 5-column technical ledger containing client data, production timestamps, and software matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects ambient blur shadows and soft digital elevations. Spatial depth is generated through physical, tactile neo-brutalist primitives:

- **Hard Contrast Offsets:** Interactive buttons and preview modules employ hard, non-blurred offsets: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0A0A0A` or `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #AB1509`.
- **Surface Inversion:** Visual hierarchy between editorial narrative and video presentation is established through whole-section canvas shifts, switching from the tactile `#FFF7D3` vanilla base into the deep-space `#0A0A0A` screening theater.
- **Physical Overlays:** Diagonal tape elements (`transform: rotate(-1.5deg)`), sharp hairline framing rules, and circular rotating graphic stamps layer across viewports to construct an authentic collage and darkroom editing console feel.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is strictly **Sharp (`0px` border-radius)** throughout the interface.

- **Cards, Frames & Input Fields:** Pure 90-degree corners create a structural, machine-tooled monitor aesthetic.
- **Buttons, Badges & Labels:** Rigid rectangular blocks echoing printed lead slugs and physical punch cards.
- **Exception Rule:** Perfectly circular geometries (`50%` radius) are reserved solely for animated production stamps, spinning director seals, and circular recording indicator nodes.

## Components

### Buttons & Call-to-Actions
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Solid `#0A0A0A` fill with `#FFF7D3` text in `Argentum Sans Black` (uppercase, tracked). Bounded by a `1.5px solid #0A0A0A` perimeter. On hover, the background snaps instantly to `#AB1509` with a hard tactile offset (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #0A0A0A`) and `-2px -2px` translation.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background with `1.5px solid #0A0A0A`. On hover, inverts to solid `#0A0A0A` with `#FFF7D3` text, or fills with `#AB1509` in darkroom mode.

### Video Cards & Media Modules
- **Frame Enclosure:** Rigid container with `1px solid #0A0A0A` or `1px solid #222222`. Supports fixed letterboxed aspect ratios (`16:9`, `9:16`, `2.39:1`).
- **Telemetry Header:** Embedded top ribbon displaying running timecode (`TC 00:02:14:08`), resolution badges (`4K PRORES 4444`), and client tags in `label-micro`.
- **Hover Reaction:** Static visual poster immediately activates a looping preview reel while the container border flashes to `#AB1509`.

### Badges, Tape & Production Stamps
- **Technical Badges:** Sharp rectangular chips bounded by `1px solid #0A0A0A`, featuring `label-micro` text in uppercase.
- **Rotating Production Seal:** Circular emblem (`50%` radius) with circular SVG path text (`MOTION DIRECTION • FRAMEFORM • ED. 2025`) continuously rotating at low RPM with a carmine status glyph at center.
- **Tactile Tape Elements:** `#E8DFC0` strips positioned across card corners to anchor metadata stamps.

### Project Ledger (Selected Works List)
- **Table / Index View:** Horizontal stacked rows partitioned by `1px solid #0A0A0A`. Contains index markers (`01`, `02`), uppercase titles in `Argentum Sans Black`, discipline tags, and release year. Hovering transforms the entire title line into `#AB1509` and positions an attached follow-cursor thumbnail.

### Form Inputs & Contact Matrix
- **Input Fields:** Flat canvas with a sharp bottom line (`2px solid #0A0A0A`) or fully boxed `1.5px` border. Active focus triggers a `#AB1509` carmine border with a blinking square block cursor.
- **Contact Display:** Monolithic hero headline in `Argentum Sans Black` uppercase ("LET'S BUILD TOGETHER"), integrated directly with high-contrast carmine CTA anchors.
